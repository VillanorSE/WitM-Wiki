# Stores and Prices

!!! abstract "Quick facts"
    - Stores sell at **115–135%** of an item's base price and buy at **65–85%**.
    - A site's supply band moves its prices: Low sells at +30%, Full sells at −15% and buys at −10%.
    - The three special components are **never sold**. You make them from salvage.
    - NPC stores never buy vehicles back.

## Which store sells what

| Store | Where | Sells | Buys |
|---|---|---|---|
| Port Goods | All 7 ports | Ice, ingots, Salvaged Ordnance, tier 2 and 3 tools, medkits, powerkits, bottles, SMLE rifle and clips | Ingots, Salvaged Ordnance, 8 basic components |
| Port Vehicles | Gray and Green ports | That side's trucks, armored cars, ships, and cargo plane, by War Level | – |
| Valencia Vehicles | Valencia | The WL1 vehicles of both sides | – |
| Airfield Vehicles | Owned airfields | That side's planes, by War Level | – |
| Depot Vehicles | Owned depots | That side's trucks, armored cars, and tanks | – |
| Factory | Plane and ground factories | The one vehicle that factory builds | – |
| Hangar | Hangars | Ju 52 (Gray) or F.222 (Green) | – |
| Manufactory | Manufactory | 16 basic components, SMLE rifle and clips | The same 16 components, 8 ores |
| Supply | Airfields, depots, factories, the large hangar | SMLE rifle and clips | 8 basic components, at 45–60% |

Vehicle prices are on [Rosters](../combat/rosters.md).

## Supply and prices

The supply band of the site the store belongs to sets a multiplier on top of the base range
([War News and Supply](../world/war-news-supply.md)):

| Band | Selling to you | Buying from you | Port Goods stock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Low | ×1.30 | ×1.00 | Half |
| Normal | ×1.00 | ×1.00 | Normal |
| Full | ×0.85 | ×0.90 | One and a half times |

Starving an enemy port makes its goods dearer and scarcer; keeping your own port Full makes it cheaper.
