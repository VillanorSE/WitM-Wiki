# In the Air

!!! abstract "Quick facts"
    - Owned airfields send out fighters, attackers and bombers that roam their side's territory.
    - From **WL4** fighters fly in **flights of three** that break off after losing two planes.
    - A plane goes down for good at **80% health**: it loses thrust and spins in.
    - Cargo planes carry supply between airfields. Shooting them down is a cheap way to hurt the enemy.
    - Attack a factory or ammo depot and a reprisal strike follows.

## Fighter flights

From War Level 4, airfields stop sending single fighters and send three planes of one type in a loose V: Re.2000 or
Re.2001 for Gray, MS.406 for Green. When a flight plane is shot down it radios the others within **6 km**. After
**two losses** the rest break off and head home, and despawn once they are 6 km away. Planes that escape give no
points.

Defenders, convoy raiders and carrier fighters fly alone and never break off.

## Crashes

When a plane drops to 80% health or its remote control is knocked out, it cuts thrust and spins. It counts as
destroyed. Planes are fragile on purpose: their cores allow very little armour.

## Cargo planes

Ju 52 and F.222 cargo planes fly from Full airfields to others of their side, loiter **5 minutes** to unload, then
leave. A delivery adds supply to the airfield, +20 territory points at the nearest anchor, and pays the side's
treasury. Plane factories also call cargo planes; their arrival completes the factory's projected blocks.

## Strikes and reprisals

| Strike | Who it targets | When |
|---|---|---|
| Base strike | Player bases inside a side's territory, owned by players at −1000 standing or worse with that side | From WL2 |
| Reprisal | The player who attacked a factory or ammo depot, and hostile players within 2 km at the time | Any War Level |

A strike is announced on War News with a countdown GPS. Each side sends at most one strike every **60 to 90
minutes**. A reprisal mark wears off after **3 hours**. Hangars and garages never trigger reprisals.
