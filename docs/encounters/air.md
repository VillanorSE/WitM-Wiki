# In the Air

!!! abstract "Quick facts"
    - Owned airfields send out fighters, attackers, and bombers that roam their side's territory.
    - At higher war levels the planes come in squadrons.
    - Cargo planes carry supply between airfields. Shooting them down is a cheap way to hurt the enemy.
    - Attack a factory or ammo depot and a reprisal strike follows.

## Fighter flights

At higher war levels, airfields stop sending single fighters and send multiple planes of one type in a loose formation. When a flight plane is shot down it radios the others within range. After enough losses, the rest of the squadron can break off and head home. Planes that escape give no points.

## Crashes

When a plane suffers enough damage or its remote control is knocked out, it crashes. It counts as
destroyed. Planes are fragile on purpose: their cores allow very little armour.

## Cargo planes

Cargo planes fly from Full airfields to others of their side, loiter for a few minutes to unload, then
leave. A delivery adds supply to the airfield, territory points at the nearest anchor of that faction, and pays the side's
treasury. Plane factories also call cargo planes; their arrival completes the factory's projected blocks.

## Strikes and reprisals

| Strike | Who it targets | When |
|---|---|---|
| Base strike | Player bases inside a side's territory, owned by players at −1000 standing or worse with that side | From WL2 |
| Reprisal | The player who attacked a factory or ammo depot, and hostile players nearby at the time | Any War Level |

A strike is announced on War News with a countdown GPS. Each side sends at most one strike every **60 to 90
minutes**. A reprisal mark wears off after a few hours. Hangars and garages never trigger reprisals.
