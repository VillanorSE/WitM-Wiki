# At Sea

!!! abstract "Quick facts"
    - **Cargo ships** (Golo class) sail fixed routes between their side's ports and are the backbone of supply.
    - **Warships** spawn inside their side's territory, wait, and engage when a target comes within range.
    - Bigger warships arrive with the War Level: destroyers from WL2, cruisers from WL4, the battleship and carriers
      from WL5.
    - Escort your side's cargo ships and enemy raiders come for them. Sink the enemy's and its ports run short.

## Cargo ships

The Italian and French Golo cargo ships run from their headquarters (La Spezia, Toulon) to the other ports of their
side and unload for **5 minutes** on arrival. A completed run:

- delivers supply to the destination port ([War News and Supply](../world/war-news-supply.md));
- adds +100 territory points at the destination anchor;
- pays the side's treasury.

A cargo ship sunk while unloading still counts as a loss.

### Convoy raids

When a player friendly to the convoy's side (standing 500 or more) stays within **1 km** of a cargo ship, the enemy
starts sending raiders against it, one wave every **18 to 25 minutes**. The raiders scale with the War Level:
corvettes and destroyers early, cruisers later, with attack planes such as the Ba.65 and FC.20 or D.520 fighters.
Without an escort there are no raids, so the convoys are a fight you choose.

### How warships behave

- They hold position until a target comes within engagement range, which grows with the ship's size, from
  about 1.5 km for small ships up to about 9 km for the largest.
- In a fight they turn to bring their broadside to bear, and break off when the target is gone.
- Idle warships stay level in the water.
- Carriers bring an escort destroyer (Spica or Bougainville) and launch planes: Re.2001 fighters from the Aquila,
  V-156-F torpedo bombers from the Béarn.
- Their guns are WeaponCore weapons from the Fletcher and Terran Titans naval packs.

Warships score heavily: sinking one costs its side reputation, territory and War Activity in proportion to its size
([Territory and War Level](../world/territory-war-level.md)).
