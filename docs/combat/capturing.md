# Capturing Vehicles

!!! abstract "Quick facts"
    - **Garages** and **hangars** hold ready-built vehicles behind a light guard. They are the intended way to get a
      good vehicle early.
    - Capture by taking a cockpit or remote control (Hostile Takeover mod).
    - Once you own a controller and no NPC still does, the **whole vehicle** becomes yours, shared with your faction.
    - Hitting garages and hangars never brings a reprisal raid.

## How a capture works

1. Deal with the guard bots and any defenders.
2. Take over a cockpit or remote control on the vehicle. Hostile Takeover handles the hacking.
3. When you own a controller and no other player or NPC still holds one on that vehicle, every block on it, subgrids
   included, passes to you, shared with your faction.

!!! warning "Ship Core after a capture"
    A captured vehicle's Ship Core may stay inactive until the world is reloaded. On a server, that means the next
    restart.

## What you can capture

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Hangars | One or two planes: fighters, attackers, bombers, torpedo bombers, cargo planes. Better types at higher War Levels. |
| Garages | One or two ground vehicles of the current War Level, for example AB41 or Panhard 178 at WL1. |
| Disabled NPC vehicles | Depot patrols and defenders stop when crippled; their turrets keep firing until you knock them out. |

Captured vehicles are the same designs that are sold in the stores, so they fit their Ship Core's limits as they come.
