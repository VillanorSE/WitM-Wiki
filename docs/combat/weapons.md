# Weapons

!!! abstract "Quick facts"
    - All guns are **WeaponCore** weapons from period weapon packs, plus a few built for this scenario.
    - Stronger guns cost **Military** or **Advanced Military Components** to build.
    - How many guns a grid may mount, and of what calibre, is set by its [Ship Core](ship-cores.md#weapon-limits).
    - Out-of-period weapons are hidden from the build menu.

## Tech costs

The Framework adds special components to strong blocks, so progression runs through salvage:

- **Basic weapons:** most are basic components only. Every 20 mm gun except the Breda 20/65 costs 1 Military
  Component.
- **Weapons:** the lighter guns cost Military Components, the strongest Advanced Military Components.
- **Ammunition:** the heaviest naval shells need **Salvaged Ordnance**.

See [Salvage and Special Components](../economy/salvage.md) for how to make them.

## Things worth knowing

- Tank main guns (the YtD CC54-38, the SC20-37, the SHA Sh.37 and the CAP 20 mm) also target soldiers on foot.
- Plane guns are fixed. On player planes they fire in Mouse Control mode, with the cockpit's fire button.
- NPC turrets run WeaponCore's own AI.
