# Getting Started

!!! abstract "Quick facts"
    - You spawn on the planet in a respawn truck: the **Fiat 626** (Italian) or the **Renault AHN** (French). Each
      has a 15-minute cooldown.
    - Pick a side early. A faction leader types `/alliance GRAY` or `/alliance GREEN`.
    - Combat, salvage and trade all lead to the same place: better Ship Cores and better vehicles.
    - Nobody starts with a warship. Advanced cores need special components you salvage, not buy.

## 1. Spawn and look around

Choose a respawn truck at the spawn screen. Both are utility trucks with no weapons worth mentioning. They carry
enough to start a small base. Drive to a road: roads link every port, airfield and depot on the planet, and
NPC supply convoys use them too.

## 2. Pick a side

New player factions start hostile to both Gray and Green. To join a side, your faction leader types one of these in
chat:

```
/alliance GRAY
/alliance GREEN
```

You become allied to that side and an enemy of the other. Allied installations let you trade and buy vehicles, and
their turrets leave you alone. See [Factions and Sides](../world/factions.md).

## 3. Get your first money and parts

- **Salvage wrecks.** Wrecked Golos, corvettes and destroyers lie partly sunk along the coasts. Their cargo holds
  carry **Battle Wreckage**, which refines into Salvage Alloy. See [Salvage](../economy/salvage.md).
- **Sell components.** Ports, the Manufactory and supply stores buy basic components such as Steel Plates and
  Construction Components. See [Stores and Prices](../economy/stores.md).
- **Hit enemy shipping.** The other side's cargo ships, cargo planes and truck convoys carry loot.

## 4. Get a better vehicle

You have three routes:

| Route | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Buy | Ports (ships, trucks, armored cars), airfields (planes), depots and factories (tanks) | Needs the right side and War Level |
| Capture | Garages and hangars hold ready-built vehicles behind a light guard | See [Capturing Vehicles](../combat/capturing.md) |
| Build | Your own design, inside a Ship Core's limits | See [Ship Cores](../combat/ship-cores.md) |

## 5. Know the rules of the war

- What you attack changes your standing with both sides. Shooting Gray costs you Gray reputation and earns a
  little with Green, and the other way round.
- Every first hit on an NPC grid pushes the global **War Level** up. Higher War Levels bring bigger ships, flights of
  fighters and better vehicles in the stores. It never goes back down on its own.
- Attack a side's factory or ammo depot and it sends a reprisal flight after you. Build a base deep in hostile
  territory with bad standing and expect air raids.

Read [Territory and War Level](../world/territory-war-level.md) next.
