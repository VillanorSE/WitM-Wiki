## Next update (in development, not on the Workshop yet)

**Ground vehicles**

- New tanks: <span class="gray-side">L6/40</span> light tank and <span class="gray-side">P26/40</span> heavy tank;
  <span class="green-side">Char B1</span> heavy tank.
- <span class="green-side">Renault R35</span> rebuilt with its historical 37 mm Puteaux SA 18 gun.
- AB41 and AB43 armored cars carry the L6/40 turret.
- Heavy tank factories, each holding two tanks.

**Weapons** (see [Weapons](../combat/weapons.md))

- Breda 20/65 mod. 35: fixed gun and AA turret.
- Puteaux SA 18 37 mm fixed gun, plus a vehicle version for the R35.
- 130 mm/45 Mle 1932 quadruple mount for the battleship Strasbourg.
- Twin Breda 38 machine guns in hull ball mounts.

**Systems**

- Supply stores at airfields, depots, factories and the large hangar: rifles and ammo for sale, basic components
  bought. See [Stores and Prices](../economy/stores.md).
- Captured NPC vehicles become wholly yours. See [Capturing Vehicles](../combat/capturing.md).
- Strasbourg on sale at Green ports from War Level 5.
- Ship Control: a Programmable Block script and a HUD throttle readout for steering large ships. See
  [Building](../economy/building.md).
- Sloped armour (TP Armour) blocks.
