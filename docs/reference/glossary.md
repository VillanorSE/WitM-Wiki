# Glossary

Anchor
:   One of the six permanent faction ports that territory grows from.

Band
:   A site's supply level: Low, Normal or Full.

Battle Wreckage
:   Ore looted from wrecks and destroyed NPC grids. Refines into Salvage Alloy.

Gray / Green
:   The two warring sides: Gray is the Axis (Italy for now), Green the Allies (France for now).

Home / Onward stock
:   A port's two supply stores: Home pays for the port itself, Onward is what ships and trucks load.

MES
:   Modular Encounters Systems, the mod that spawns and runs every NPC in the scenario.

Player / NPC prefab
:   Every hull exists twice: a `Player-WW2-` version that is sold, stolen or captured, and an `NPC-WW2-` version with
    AI that fights.

Reprisal
:   A strike sent after a player who attacked a factory or ammo depot.

Salvage Alloy
:   The ingot that unlocks the special components.

Salvaged Ordnance
:   An ingot looted from bigger kills, needed for the heaviest naval shells.

Ship Core
:   The block that sets a grid's class, limits and top speed.

Special components
:   Industrial, Military and Advanced Military Components. Made from salvage, never sold.

Territory
:   The circle around each anchor, 5 to 35 km, that decides who owns airfields and depots and where NPCs spawn.

Traders
:   The neutral faction at Valencia.

War Activity
:   The hidden counter behind the War Level; it goes up each time an NPC grid is first attacked.

War Level (WL)
:   The global war intensity, 1 to 5. Higher levels bring stronger NPCs and better vehicles in stores.
